= Hot-swap a fan in your AFF A250 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Replace a failed fan module in your AFF A250 system to maintain proper cooling and prevent performance issues. Fan modules are hot-swappable and can be replaced without shutting down the system.
