= Replace or install a mezzanine card in your AFF A250 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Replace a failed mezzanine card in your AFF A250 system or install a new one to expand connectivity options. The replacement process involves removing the cables and SFP or QSFP modules, replacing the card, and recabling.
