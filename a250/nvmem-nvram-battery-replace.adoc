= Replace the NV battery in your AFF A250 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Replace the NVMEM battery in your AFF A250 system when it begins to lose charge or fails, as it preserves critical system data during power outages. The replacement process involves removing the controller module, replacing the battery, and reinstalling the controller module.
