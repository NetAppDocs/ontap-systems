= Review requirements before replacing an AFF A20, AFF A30, or AFF A50 chassis
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Before replacing the chassis of your AFF A20, AFF A30, or AFF A50 storage system, verify that you meet the necessary requirements for a successful replacement. This includes verifying that all other components in the system are functioning properly, verifying that you have the correct replacement chassis, and verifying that you have the necessary tools.
