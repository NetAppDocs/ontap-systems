= Cable an NS224 shelf to your AFF A30, AFF A50, AFF C30, or AFF C60
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Cable your NS224 shelf to your AFF A30, AFF A50, AFF C30, or AFF C60 system so that each shelf has two connections to each controller in the HA pair.

You can hot-add up to two NS224 shelves to an AFF A30, AFF C30, AFF A50, or AFF C60 HA pair when additional storage (to the internal shelf) is needed.

include::../_include/cable-30-50-60-hot-add-shelf.adoc[]



.What's next

If you disabled automatic drive assignment as part of the preparation for this procedure, you need to manually assign drive ownership and then reenable automatic drive assignment, if needed. Go to link:hot-add-aff-complete.html[Complete the hot-add].

Otherwise, you are done with the hot-add shelf procedure.
