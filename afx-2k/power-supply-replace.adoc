= Replace a power supply - AFX 2K
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Replace an AC power supply unit (PSU) in your AFX 2K storage system when it fails or becomes faulty, ensuring that your system continues to receive the required power for stable operation. The replacement process involves disconnecting the target PSU, unplugging the power cable, removing the faulty PSU and installing the replacement PSU, and then reconnecting it to the power source.
