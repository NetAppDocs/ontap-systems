= Review requirements for replacing the boot media in your AFF A400 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Before replacing the boot media in your AFF A400 system, verify that you meet the necessary requirements for a successful replacement. This includes having a USB flash drive formatted to FAT32 with sufficient storage for the ONTAP recovery image and determining whether your replacement is nondisruptive or disruptive.
