= AFF A400 chassis replacement workflow
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Get started with replacing the chassis of your AFF A400 system by shutting down the controllers, moving the hardware to the replacement chassis, and completing the system restoration.

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-1.png[One]link:chassis-replace-shutdown.html[Shut down the controllers]
[role="quick-margin-para"]
Shut down the controllers to perform chassis maintenance safely.

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-2.png[Two]link:chassis-replace-move-hardware.html[Replace the chassis]
[role="quick-margin-para"]
Move the fans, drives, and controller modules from the impaired chassis to the replacement chassis.

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-3.png[Three]link:chassis-replace-complete-system-restore-rma.html[Complete the chassis replacement]
[role="quick-margin-para"]
Complete the replacement by verifying the HA state, switching back aggregates if needed, and returning the failed chassis to NetApp.
