= Replace a PCIe or mezzanine card in an AFF A400 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Replace a PCIe or mezzanine card in your AFF A400 system when the card fails or requires an upgrade. The replacement process involves disconnecting the cables and SFP or QSFP modules, replacing the card, and recabling.
