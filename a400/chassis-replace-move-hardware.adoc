= Replace the chassis in your AFF A400 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Replace the chassis of your AFF A400 system when a hardware failure requires it. The replacement process involves moving the fans, drives, and controller modules from the impaired chassis to the replacement chassis.
