= Hot-swap a power supply in an AFF A800 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Replace a power supply in your AFF A800 system when it fails or becomes faulty. Power supplies are redundant and hot-swappable, so you do not have to shut down the controller to perform this replacement.
