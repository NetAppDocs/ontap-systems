= Review requirements before replacing the controller in an AFF A800 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Before replacing the controller of your AFF A800 system, verify that you meet the necessary requirements for a successful replacement. This includes verifying that all other components in the system are functioning properly, verifying that you have the correct replacement controller, and saving the controller's console output to a text log file.

include::../_include/800_controller_replace_requirements.adoc[]

.What's next?

After you've reviewed the requirements to replace your AFF A800 controller, you need to link:controller-replace-shutdown.html[shut down the impaired controller].
