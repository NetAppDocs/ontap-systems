= Review the requirements for replacing the controller in your AFF A150 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Before replacing the controller in your AFF A150 system, ensure you meet the necessary requirements for a successful replacement. This includes verifying that all drive shelves are working properly, that the healthy controller can take over the impaired controller, and that you select the correct replacement procedure for your version of ONTAP.
