= Replace the chassis in your AFF A150 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Replace the chassis of your AFF A150 system when a hardware failure requires it. The replacement process involves moving the power supplies, drives, and controller modules from the impaired chassis to the replacement chassis.
