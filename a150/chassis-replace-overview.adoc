= Review requirements before replacing the chassis in your AFF A150 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Before replacing the chassis in your AFF A150 system, confirm that you meet the necessary requirements for a successful replacement. This includes verifying that all other components in the system are functioning properly and understanding that the procedure is disruptive and requires shutting down both controllers.
