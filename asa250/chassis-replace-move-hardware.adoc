= Replace the chassis in your ASA A250 system
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Replace the chassis in your ASA A250 system when a hardware failure requires it. The replacement process involves removing the controllers and power supply units (PSUs), removing the drives, installing the replacement chassis, and reinstalling the chassis components.
