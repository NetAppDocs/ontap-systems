= Review requirements before replacing your FAS70 or FAS90 chassis
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Before replacing the chassis in your FAS70 or FAS90 system, verify that you meet the necessary requirements for a successful replacement. This includes confirming that all other components in the system are functioning properly, verifying that you have local administrator credentials for ONTAP, the correct replacement chassis, and the necessary tools.
